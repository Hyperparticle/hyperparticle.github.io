You can find links to major projects and publications I have worked on below.

- [rekursiv.ai](https://rekursiv.ai/) - Building teams of AI Scientists to autonomously create knowledge.
- [Dream Machine](https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine) - Generative video models for Luma AI's core product.
- [VideoPoet](https://sites.research.google/videopoet/) - A multimodal LLM for zero-shot video and audio generation; ICML 2024 Best Paper.
- [MoViNets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.11511) - Mobile video networks for efficient, realtime video action recognition.
- [UDify](https://github.com/Hyperparticle/udify) - A single multilingual model for universal dependency parsing across 75 languages.
- [Integrated Multimodal Perception (IMP)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06324) - A Mixture-of-Experts model for image, video, audio, and text perception.
- [LemmaTag](https://github.com/Hyperparticle/LemmaTag) - A neural model for joint part-of-speech tagging and lemmatization.
- [One Pixel Attack Keras](https://github.com/Hyperparticle/one-pixel-attack-keras) - A Keras implementation showing how changing one pixel can fool convolutional networks.
- [Nodulus](/nodulus/) - An open source mobile puzzle game.
- [Graph NLU](https://github.com/Hyperparticle/graph-nlu) - A dialog-system experiment using graph databases for natural language understanding.
